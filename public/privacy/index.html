Privacy

This site is a static portfolio. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from a third party.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms, no comment fields, and no client-side storage. No cookie is set and no script of mine reports anything about your visit — there is nothing to report to.

The only interactive elements are links: the email address opens your own mail client, the résumé is a static PDF you can choose to open, and the project links leave for GitHub.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub and its content delivery network process the technical details a browser sends with a request — IP address, user agent, requested path — in order to serve it and to protect against abuse. That processing happens on GitHub's infrastructure under GitHub's privacy statement. I do not receive, keep, or analyse those logs.

If you email me

Writing to kushalmam06@gmail.com sends the message to a Gmail account. I use it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and for nothing else. I do not add it to a mailing list, and I do not pass it to anyone.

Links to other sites

GitHub, LinkedIn, and the project repositories are separate services with their own privacy policies. Once you follow a link, this page's promises no longer apply.

Your rights

If you have emailed me and would like your message deleted, ask and I will delete it. Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, or object to processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Email the address above to make a request.

Because this site collects nothing, there is nothing here to access, export, or erase.
